Privacy Policy

IRLS HOLDINGS LLC d/b/a NJ Shore Clean — Last updated October 2026

Who we are

NJ Shore Clean, operated by IRLS HOLDINGS LLC ("we," "us"), provides turnover cleaning services for vacation rental owners in New Jersey. Contact: robert@thoughtcollect.com.

What we collect

  • Contact information: your name and mobile phone number, when you text us or provide them.
  • Service information: property address, property details (bedrooms/bathrooms), scheduling dates, rental calendar links you share, and service notes.
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Message history: the contents of SMS conversations with us, so we can provide service and keep records.
  • Job documentation: photos of serviced properties taken by our crew.

How we use it

We use this data to provide quotes, schedule and perform cleaning services, send service confirmations and completion notifications with photos, process payments, and respond to inquiries. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes.

SMS/text messaging

When you text our number, you consent to receive conversational replies about quotes, scheduling, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers necessary to operate (e.g., Twilio for messaging, Stripe for payments) and as required by law. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention & your rights

We retain records as needed for service, legal, and tax purposes. To request access to or deletion of your data, email robert@thoughtcollect.com or text us.
